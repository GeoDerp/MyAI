# Acceptable Use Policy

This project provides tooling that uses large language models and external retrieval services. To reduce harm and protect users, please follow these rules:

- Do not use the agent to generate content that is illegal, sexually explicit, violent, or that facilitates wrongdoing.
- Do not use the tool to de-anonymize individuals or to perform invasive surveillance.
- When producing research reports that could influence decisions (medical, legal, financial), surface the provenance for all claims and require human review before publication.
- Respect copyright: cite sources and avoid redistributing full-text content unless the source allows it.

If you're unsure whether a use is permitted, err on the side of caution and consult project maintainers.
